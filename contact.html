Frequently Asked Questions

Essential details regarding technical evaluation, campus access, tuition installments, and curriculum pacing.

How soon will an advisor contact me?

expand_more

Once your lead intake form is verified, an Engineering Advisory Specialist connects via phone or WhatsApp within 24 business hours (Monday through Saturday, 9:00 AM – 8:00 PM IST).

They review your current technical prerequisites and career targets before scheduling your personalized curriculum briefing.

Can I visit the Bareilly or Bengaluru campus in person?

expand_more

Yes. Both the Bareilly Engineering Campus and the Bengaluru Technology Office welcome prospective engineers for scheduled campus walkthroughs and hardware lab inspections.

We recommend submitting the enquiry form first so an advisor can register an official campus visitor pass and allocate dedicated mentor time.

What are the payment options and installments?

expand_more

We provide zero-cost EMI plans spread over 6, 9, or 12 monthly installments through approved institutional NBFC partners.

Upfront single-term tuition fees are eligible for an academic merit concession of up to 10% upon successful evaluation in the entrance diagnostic challenge.

How do I take the technical diagnostic assessment?

expand_more

After your intake form is processed, your email will receive a secure portal invite to the browser-based assessment.

The 15-minute diagnostic consists of 12 logic and algorithmic comprehension challenges, plus foundational networking/systems questions depending on your selected programme track.
