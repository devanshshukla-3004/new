Upon RSVP confirmation, you receive a secured magic token link. 15 minutes before the session starts, clicking the link initializes an isolated Docker container running on our Bareilly/Bengaluru edge clusters with pre-installed toolkits (Nmap, Wireshark CLI, Suricata, Metasploit).
